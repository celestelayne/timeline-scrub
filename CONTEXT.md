# Trinidad & Tobago Timeline

A scrubbable, full-screen timeline of the cultural history of Trinidad & Tobago (4000 BC to 1940), where one position along the timeline selects the content shown.

## Language

**Timeline Event**:
A dated entry on the timeline: a moment, or the start of a span. Cultures and periods (for example Saladoid) are Timeline Events too.
_Avoid_: Entry, data point, milestone

**Playhead**:
The fixed marker at the horizontal centre of the track. The timeline moves under it; it never moves.

**Gap**:
The number of years between two consecutive Timeline Events' years. A span's end year is ignored. Gaps of 100 years or more are labelled on the track.
_Avoid_: Interval, jump

**Active Timeline Event**:
The Timeline Event nearest the Playhead. It is the only one whose content is shown, and the only one drawn filled.
_Avoid_: Current event, selected event, hovered event
